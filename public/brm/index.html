Promotion vs free listing

Promotion on — visits jump. Promotion off — they fall.

A real project on Reelly: months on a free listing, then 12 days of promotion, then a free listing again. Promotion raises how often agents see your project; how many open it depends on your offer — price, district, payment plan. So the uplift ranges from 2× to 7×, typically 4×.
